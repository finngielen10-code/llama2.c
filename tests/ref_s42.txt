Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play in the park with her friends. One day, while playing, she saw a boy crying. She went over to him and asked him what was wrong. The boy said he had lost his ball and couldn't find it anywhere.
Lily felt compassionate and wanted to help the boy. She asked him where he last had it and he pointed to a bench. She went to the bench and found the ball under it. The boy was so happy and thanked her.
Lily felt happy too because she helped someone. She realized that even if someone looks scary, they can still be kind and compassionate. She learned that helping others can make them feel good and make you feel good too.
